Controls

Toggle

The knob slides 20 px in 180 ms and stretches a little on the way; the track changes colour with it. The “Tungi rejim” switch cross-fades the whole screen in 250 ms.

Text field

Focus: the edge turns violet in 120 ms. Right: green with a small punch. Not right: red with a shake (350 ms).

Segmented switch

The white pill slides under the chosen word in 180 ms; the words swap colour in 120 ms.

Rating and feedback form

Number: press, then violet with a punch. YUBORISH: the form shrinks up and away (220 ms), then a green “Rahmat!” toast.

Reminder time picker

Drag, scroll or tap a time. It follows the finger, then snaps to the nearest time in 220 ms; the chosen time grows and turns violet.
