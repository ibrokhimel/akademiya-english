Every lesson component, built to the Pencil sizes, with its real motion. Click and press things. The numbers in grey are the timings the app will copy.

Buttons

Main button

Press: face drops 4 px in 60 ms, springs back in 140 ms. Becomes ready: colour 120 ms + one shine (600 ms), again every 5 s. Tapped while grey: small shake.

The other buttons

Same press. No shine. Small buttons drop 3 px.

Answers

Answer tiles

Pick: violet in 120 ms, lifts 2 px. Check → right: green, punch 1.08 (200 ms), pop. Not right: red, shake 350 ms; the right one turns green, no pop.

Picture tiles

Same as answer tiles. The pop comes from the middle of the picture.

Tile Builder

Tap: the tile flies to the line on a small arc (260 ms) and lands with a squash; the others close the gap (180 ms). Right: the line turns green left to right, 60 ms apart.

Tap the mistake

Pick: violet. Right: a line strikes the word (200 ms), the fix drops in from above, pop. Not right: red + shake, the real mistake turns green.

Tap the gap

Empty gaps breathe slowly (1.2 s). Pick: fills violet. Right: the gap closes into the word, which lands with a squash and a pop.

Lesson frame

Progress bar and combo

Grows in 320 ms with a small overshoot and a shine. The combo number bumps on every right answer. From 5 in a row the bar is gold. At 100 % a pop at the end of the bar.

Speaking and audio

Mic button

Your turn: a ring breathes out every 1.2 s. Press: turns coral, rings follow the voice. Then three dots while checking, then green with a check that draws itself. The words light up one by one, 90 ms apart.

Audio buttons
