Where can he be touched?

Touch him

  • Hold ~0.3 s: he hops onto your finger. Drag to carry (he swings, grips, looks where you go). Shake him: dizzy.
  • Let go: he falls, lands, shakes it off, walks home. Throw him: he tumbles, lands on his feet, smug blink.
  • Drop on the card: sits and peeks. Drop by the flame: warms his hands. Drag off the screen: he peeks back in.
  • Tap: poke (1 wobble, 2 raised lid, 3 pushes you away, 4 turns his back). Double-tap: a trick.
  • Rub (press and move straight away): petting. Mouse wheel / two fingers: pinch stretch.
  • Drag a word from the top onto him: he eats it.
  • Move the pointer anywhere: his eyes follow.
