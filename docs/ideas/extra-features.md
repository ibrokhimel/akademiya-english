## Voice privacy promise (locked 2026-10-09 — owner's rule)

Shown to users at consent: **"Your voice recordings are private. Only you can listen to them —
nobody else can."**

What this commits us to:
- Recordings are stored privately and can only be played back through the learner's own account.
- **Nobody else listens:** not other users, not parents, not teachers, not our team.
- Recordings are never shared, sold, or used in marketing; parent reports (later) never include audio.
- The learner can delete their recordings at any time (Settings).
- Analytics and testing use numbers only (e.g. minutes spoken), never audio.
- ⚠️ Honest wording note: the phone's speech recognition (Apple / Google) may process speech to turn it into
  text. The consent text must say this truthfully, e.g. "Your phone's speech recognition turns your voice
  into text so we can check your answer." Exact wording is checked in roadmap #33 (privacy).
