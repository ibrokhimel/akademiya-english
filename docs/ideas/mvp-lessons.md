## Recycling

- Café reuses Hello / Thank you / Goodbye; Shopping reuses numbers, "How much…?", "Can I pay by card?", "please".
- Each Checkpoint mixes in phrases from all earlier units; review items in every lesson come from earlier lessons.

**After the MVP a learner can say 45+ phrases:** introduce themselves, order in a café, ask prices, pay,
and buy clothes in the right colour and size.
