## Three questions progress must answer

| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| "How far am I?" | Level + stage + path |
| "What can I actually do?" | "Things I can say" list |
| "Am I improving?" | Statistics now; voice comparison and weekly summary after the MVP |

## MVP (locked)

**Levels**
- Levels 0–4 with stages (First steps → Survive → Live → Connect → Achieve).
- A level is finished when all its units' Checkpoints are passed.
- Progress bar to the next level: "Level 0 · 2 of 3 units".
- Level-up celebration (MVP has Level 0 → early Level 1, so testers see one level-up).

**Progress screen**
- Level, stage and progress bar to the next level
- Prediction: "At your pace: Level 1 by 2 Nov"
- Streak 🔥 + streak freeze ❄️
- "You can say 14 things" → opens the list
- Statistics: lessons · phrases · minutes spoken · days active
- Unit trophies (👋 Hello ✓ · ☕ Café ✓ · 🛍️ …)

**"Things I can say" list**
- Grouped by unit.
- Each phrase: ▶ native audio · ▶ the learner's own voice **where one was recorded** (recordings come from
  random speaking questions, only with permission).
- Tap a phrase → practise it again (quick Echo).

**Voice recordings** — with permission, recorded during random speaking questions and saved for later
comparison. The learner hears their own voice through the progress screen or a random "compare?" moment —
not on the lesson celebration card.

## After the MVP

- Same phrase recorded again after ~30 days + random "compare?" moment
- End-of-week summary
- Speaking confidence score (needs AI)
- App level certificate (clearly not official)
- **Achievements — the actual list is decided after the MVP**, based on how real learners use the app.

(first lesson, unit master, level up, things you can say) · accuracy (perfect lessons, no hints) · habit

certificate) · scenes · pet · hidden surprises. Achievements would pay Apples 🍎, some hidden, some tiered.

**Other progress ideas:** phrase strength bars (needs FSRS) · "these 5 need practice" · activity calendar ·
