# LOCKED — Course Map (English app)

**Status:** Locked as the plan · **Date locked:** 2026-10-09 · **Roadmap item:** #19
**Note:** Units 4–48 are a plan, refined later. The MVP contains the **first units only** (Level 0).
**Related:** [teaching-methodology.md](teaching-methodology.md) · [product-definition.md](product-definition.md)

**Totals:** ~48 units × 5 lessons ≈ 240 lessons, plus a Checkpoint (later a Scene) after each unit.

## Rules the map follows

- **Spiral:** old topics come back deeper (café → restaurant → complaints; shopping → clothes and returns).
- **Recycling:** each unit reuses earlier words.
- **Grammar only when a situation needs it**, never as a unit title.
- **One Uzbek-speaker trap focus per unit**, feeding the mistake engine.
- **Everyday topics for every age (13+).**

## Level 0 — First steps (Pre-A1) · 3 units · = the MVP

Numbers are taught inside Café and Shopping (prices), so no separate "Numbers" unit.

| # | Unit | Learner can… | Patterns | Uzbek trap focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 👋 Hello! | Greet, say their name, where they're from, how they are | I'm…, my name is…, I'm from… | Missing "am/is" ("I Aziz") |
| 2 | ☕ At the café | Order food and drinks, ask the price, pay, numbers 1–20 | I'd like…, Can I have…, How much…? | Plural -s ("two coffee"), a/an |
| 3 | 🛍️ Shopping | Ask for things, prices, colours, sizes, numbers to 100 | Do you have…?, this/that, It's… | this/these, plural -s, word order |

Draft lesson skills (finalised in #20):
- **Hello:** greet · say your name · say where you're from · ask "How are you?" · say goodbye
- **Café:** order a drink · order food · ask the price · say what you don't want · pay and say thanks
- **Shopping:** ask "Do you have…?" · ask the price · choose a colour · ask for a size · say "I'll take it"
