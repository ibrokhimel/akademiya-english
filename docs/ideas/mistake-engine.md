## How a mistake is handled (Duolingo-style, same in every exercise)

1. Show the **correct answer** with audio.
2. If it is a known Uzbek trap → **one-line Uzbek explanation** (💡). Otherwise only the correct answer.
3. **Continue** → next question.
4. The mistake comes back **at the end of the lesson**, again until answered correctly.

No "wrong", no "not yet", no red screen, no hearts.

## The 9 Uzbek-speaker traps

| ID | Trap | ❌ Uzbek speakers say | ✅ Correct | Lessons | 💡 Uzbek explanation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Missing "am" | I Aziz · I from Tashkent · I fine | I'm Aziz | 2, 3, 4 | O'zbek tilida «-man» qo'shimchasi bor: *Men Azizman*. Ingliz tilida u alohida so'z — **am**: *I am Aziz → I'm Aziz.* |
| T2 | a / an | A orange juice · Coffee, please | An orange juice · A coffee | 6, 7, 13 | O'zbek tilida artikl yo'q. Bitta narsa oldidan **a** qo'yiladi: *a coffee*. So'z unli tovush bilan boshlansa — **an**: *an orange juice.* |
| T3 | Plural after numbers | Two coffee | Two coffees | 8 | O'zbekchada «ikkita qahva» deymiz. Ingliz tilida birdan ko'p bo'lsa **-s** qo'shiladi: *two coffees.* |
| T4 | "no" for negation | I no want sugar | I don't want sugar / No sugar, please | 9 | O'zbekchada inkor fe'lga qo'shiladi: *xohlamayman*. Ingliz tilida **don't** ishlatiladi: *I don't want sugar.* Qisqasi: *No sugar, please.* |
| T5 | Thanks you | Thanks you! | Thank you! / Thanks! | 10 | **Thank you** yoki **Thanks** deymiz. *Thanks you* — ikkalasini aralashtirmang. |
| T6 | Question without "Do" | You have water? | Do you have water? | 11 | O'zbekchada savol «-mi» bilan yasaladi: *Sizda suv bormi?* Ingliz tilida savol boshiga **Do** qo'yiladi: *Do you have water?* |
| T7 | Missing "is" | How much this? | How much is this? | 12 | O'zbekchada «Bu qancha?» deymiz — fe'lsiz. Ingliz tilida **is** kerak: *How much is this?* |
| T8 | very vs too | It's very big ("doesn't fit") | It's too big | 14 | O'zbekchada «juda katta» ikki ma'noda. Ingliz tilida: **very big** — juda katta; **too big** — haddan tashqari katta, to'g'ri kelmaydi. |
| T9 | fifteen vs fifty (listening) | Hears 50 instead of 15 | fifTEEN / FIFty | 15 | **fifTEEN** (15) — urg'u oxirida, cho'ziq «iː». **FIFty** (50) — urg'u boshida. |

T9 is taught through E6 What Happened? (prices), not Tap the Mistake.

**Changes from the first lesson draft:** Lesson 10 trap "pay with card" → **"Thanks you"** ("pay with a card" is
correct English). Lesson 12 trap "this / these" → **"How much this?"** ("these" is not taught yet).
