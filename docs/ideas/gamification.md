## The rule

Gamification rewards **real learning**, not tapping.
**The main score is the number of things you can SAY** ("Things I can say" count) — never spent.

## MVP gamification package (locked)

| # | Element | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Things I can say" counter | Main score: "You can say 14 things in English" |
| 2 | "New thing you can SAY" card | End of every lesson (no own-voice playback here) |
| 3 | Simple streak 🔥 | Days in a row with at least 1 lesson |
| 4 | Streak freeze | 1 free freeze per week, used automatically on a missed day |
| 5 | Lesson result screen | Phrases learned, accuracy, "Perfect lesson ✨" with no mistakes |
| 6 | Combo in a lesson | "5 in a row! 🔥" for consecutive correct answers |
| 7 | Sounds, vibration, small animations | On correct answers and lesson finish |
| 8 | Path that fills up | Finished lessons/units light up; the next one glows |
| 9 | Unit Checkpoint trophy | "You can now handle a café visit ☕" |
| 10 | Reaction faces at the Checkpoint | 🙂 / 😄 / 🤩 based on performance (locked reactions, no scene needed) |
| 11 | Level-up moment | Celebration when moving Level 0 → Level 1 |
| 12 | Updated prediction | "At your pace: A2 by 15 March" on the progress screen |

**Progress / user statistics (MVP):** lessons done, phrases learned ("Things I can say"), streak,
**minutes spoken out loud** (measured from microphone time), prediction.

Not in the MVP: milestone badges / achievements, XP, leagues, friends, quests, shop.

## Currency and shop (locked — launches AFTER the MVP, together with the pet)

- A **separate currency — Apples 🍎** — earned and spent in the shop. It is not the score.
- **At launch: earned only by learning** — lessons, perfect lessons, speaking out loud (speaking pays
  more than tapping), Checkpoints, streak milestones, level-ups. Example amounts are tuned in testing.
- **Later:** the currency can also be **bought with real money**, and **Premium users earn more** of it.
- **Never sold:** skips — no "skip this lesson / Checkpoint", nothing that makes learning optional.

**Shop item ideas:** extra streak freezes · app colour themes · profile frames / avatar items ·
🐾 pet outfits and accessories (best item; e.g. an Uzbek doppi) · 🐾 pet corner decorations ·
scene character outfits · bonus fun content (short stories, songs) as long as the core course never needs coins.

## After the MVP (planned)

XP + weekly leagues · friends and friend streaks · daily quests · full achievement system ·
scene reactions · weekly summary + voice comparison · 🐾 pet growth ·
🐾 pet accessories (shop) · streak repair within 24 h · shareable cards (Telegram / Instagram).

## Never

Hearts / energy · paying to skip learning · guilt-trip notifications.
