## 1. The method in one sentence

> Learners **hear** real English, **notice** the pattern (explained in Uzbek), **say** it out loud, and
> **use** it in a small real-life task — and the app keeps bringing it back so they never forget it.

(Task-based learning + listen-first input + speaking from day one + comparison with the learner's own
language + spaced repetition and constant recycling.)

## 2. Eight teaching principles

| # | Principle | In practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Speak from day one** | Every lesson ends with the learner saying something out loud — even the first ("Hello, I'm Aziz") |
| 2 | **Listen before reading** | New English is heard first; text appears after |
| 3 | **Phrases before grammar** | Learn "I'd like a…" as a ready-made phrase; grammar later, if needed |
| 4 | **Every lesson = one real ability** | Each lesson ends with "You can now ORDER A DRINK", not "Lesson 12 done" |
| 5 | **Uzbek as a bridge that fades** | Lots of Uzbek at Level 0, almost none by Level 3 |
| 6 | **Fix the mistakes Uzbek causes** | he/she, "three book", a/the, word order, "th" — explained by comparing with Uzbek |
| 7 | **Recycle everything** | New lessons and units keep reusing earlier words and phrases; reviews bring old material back |
| 8 | **Mistakes never say "wrong"** | Duolingo-style: show the right answer, move on, and the mistake comes back at the end of the lesson |

## 3. Lessons are not "daily" (changed 2026-10-09)

- A lesson is a **short unit of learning (~3–5 min)** — not "the lesson for today".
- The learner can **do as many lessons in a row as they want**, like Duolingo. They choose to continue or stop.
- The app **does not ask how many minutes a day** the learner wants to practise.
- Core promise reworded: **"Every lesson leaves you with one new thing you can actually SAY."**
- The signup prediction uses a default pace and then **updates from the learner's real pace**
  ("At your current pace you'll reach A2 by 15 March").

## 4. Course structure

```
LEVEL (0–4)
 └── STAGE                 e.g. "Survive"
      └── UNIT             one theme, e.g. "At the café"
           └── LESSON      one can-say skill, ~3–5 min
                └── EXERCISES  5–8 short exercises
```

### Course stages (#7 — locked)

| Stage | Level | Learner can… | Example units |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First steps** | 0 (Pre-A1) | Say their first words and sentences | Hello · Numbers · Me & my family |
| **Survive** | 1 (A1) | Handle simple everyday situations | Café · Shopping · Directions · Transport |
| **Live** | 2 (A2) | Talk about their life, plans and problems | Work & study · Weekend · At the doctor |
| **Connect** | 3 (B1) | Give opinions, tell stories, small talk | Stories · Opinions · Phone calls · Complaints |
| **Achieve** | 4 (B2) | Reach their goal | IELTS Speaking · Interviews · Study abroad |

Rough size (to confirm in course map #19): ~5 units at Level 0, 10–12 per level for Levels 1–3,
~10 + goal tracks at Level 4.

### One unit

```
UNIT: At the café ☕
Lesson 1 → You can ORDER A DRINK
Lesson 2 → You can ASK THE PRICE
Lesson 3 → You can ASK ABOUT FOOD
Lesson 4 → You can CHANGE YOUR ORDER
Lesson 5 → You can PAY AND SAY GOODBYE
Unit end → 🔁 CHECKPOINT (MVP) / 🎬 SCENE (after MVP)
```

- **MVP:** the unit ends with a **Checkpoint** — a mixed speaking review of the whole unit **plus
  phrases from earlier units** → "You can now handle a café visit ☕".
- **After the MVP:** the unit ends with the Scene.

## 5. Recycling and revision (Duolingo-style)

- **Every new lesson reuses earlier material**: new phrases are built from words the learner already
  knows ("I'd like **a coffee**" from Unit 2 returns in Unit 3 as "Do you have **a coffee** in a smaller size?").
- **Every unit reuses earlier units' words and phrases** in its exercises, examples and the Checkpoint.
- **Review items are mixed into every lesson** (words due for review + past mistakes), not a separate chore.
- **Practice / review lessons** are available any time to revise old units (FSRS decides what is due).
- Every phrase climbs the ladder **recognise → rebuild → recall → use** and drops back when it is being forgotten.

## 6. One lesson — the recipe "Hear → Notice → Say → Use" (#6 — locked)

| Step | Goal | Exercises (MVP ones in **bold**) |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Hear** | Meet the new phrase by ear | **E1 Meet It**, **E6 What Happened?**, E7, E8 |
| **2. Notice** | Understand the pattern and the Uzbek-speaker trap | **E13 Tap the Mistake**, E12 Find the Pattern, E5 |
| **3. Say** | Produce it: build, repeat, say from memory | **E4 Tile Builder**, **E9 Echo Line**, **E2 Say What You See**, E3, E10, E11, E14 |
| **4. Use** | Use it in a tiny situation | **E2 with a situation task** (MVP); E15/E16 Quick Reply after MVP |
| **🎉 Finish** | "New thing you can SAY" card | — |

- Old material (review + past mistakes) is mixed into steps 3 and 4.
- Never more than 2–3 of the same exercise in a row; each exercise under 30 seconds.

## 7. Mistakes — Duolingo-style (changed 2026-10-09)

- The app **never says "wrong" or "not yet"**.
- After a mistake: show the **correct answer** (with audio) and, when it's a known Uzbek-speaker
  pattern, **one line in Uzbek explaining why** — then the learner **moves to the next exercise**.
- **Every mistake goes to the end of the lesson** and is asked again until it is answered correctly.
- Mistakes are also saved for later reviews.
- Correct answers: short sound + light vibration, sometimes a character's smile; no generic praise.
- Speaking "almost": green/orange words; tap an orange word to hear it.

## 8. How each skill is taught

| Skill | How |
|---|---|
| Speaking | The centre: repeat → build → say from memory → use in a situation → (later) conversation and Scene |
| Listening | Always first; short real-sounding dialogues; text revealed after answering |
| Pronunciation | Sounds Uzbek speakers find hard (th, w/v, ship/sheep); word-by-word green/orange feedback |
| Vocabulary | Phrases, not word lists; most-used words first; grouped by situation; picture + audio |
| Grammar | No lectures; examples → find the pattern → 2-line Uzbek explanation; no grammar terms at Levels 0–1 |
| Reading | Phrases at Levels 0–1; short texts and messages from Level 2 |
| Writing | Not a main goal; only 1–3 word typing; tiles instead of long sentences |

## 9. How teaching changes by level

| | Level 0 | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 | Level 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Uzbek support | Everything | Instructions | Explanations only | On tap | Tricky grammar only |
| What you say | Words → 2-word phrases | Short phrases | Full sentences | 2–3 sentences, opinions | Longer answers (IELTS style) |
| Main exercises | Meet It, Echo, tiles, pictures | + Say What You See, Fix It | + Quick Reply, Find the Pattern | + Free Reply, Scenes | Scenes, IELTS practice |
| Help | Lots of hints | Hints on request | Fewer | Rare | None |
| Grammar | None by name | Patterns only | Short rules | Rules + comparisons | Fine details |

## 10. Proof of progress (#9 — locked, owner's idea)

**Voice recordings (updated 2026-10-09)**
- **Permission first:** before anything is recorded, the app asks whether the learner allows their voice
  to be recorded (with the privacy promise: only they can listen). No permission → nothing is recorded.
- Recordings are made **during a random speaking question** inside a lesson — not a separate task — and
  **saved on the server**.
- **After about 30 days** the same phrase is recorded again in the same way, so the comparison is fair.
- The learner hears their own voice **only at a random moment** (shown after a finished lesson, never
  interrupting one: "Want to hear how much you've improved?" → then vs now) **or through the progress
  screen**. It is **not** on the lesson celebration card.
- This repeats over time (a "voice timeline").
- Needs **clear consent**, a way to **delete recordings**, and extra care for under-18s (roadmap #33).

**End-of-week summary**
- At the end of each week the app shows **everything learned that week**: number of words, phrases,
  "can-say" skills, lessons, minutes spoken, mistakes fixed.

**Suggested additions (not yet confirmed by the owner)**
- Shareable summary card (Spotify-Wrapped style) for Telegram/Instagram.
- "Most improved" line — a sound or phrase that got better.
- "Your mistake of the week" — the Uzbek-speaker trap fixed most.
- Next-week preview — "Next: you'll learn to ask for directions".
- Milestone recordings at each new level.
- Total "minutes spoken out loud" counter.

**MVP:** basic progress (lessons, phrases learned) + "Things I can say" list. Permission + first recordings
during random questions can be collected in the MVP; comparison and weekly summary may follow.

## 11. What we don't do

No grammar lectures or terms for beginners · no word lists out of context · no translating long texts ·
no long passive videos · no long typing on a phone · no punishing mistakes (no hearts/energy, no "wrong") ·
no unchecked AI content · no "how many minutes a day?" question.
